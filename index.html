LicenseQuest

Every plate has a home. Collect illustrated regions, explore rarity and discover roadside achievements with Decker and the crew.

Need a hand?

Email bprendie@icloud.com. Include your device model, iOS version and what happened. Please omit private location details unless needed.

Your collection

Collections and trips are stored on your device. There is no account or automatic cross-device sync. Use the app's undo controls for accidental entries. Avoid deleting the app when troubleshooting because this may remove local records.

Permissions are optional

Touch logging and manual home/trip selection work without location, speech or Bluetooth access. Change permissions in iOS Settings. GPS mileage and background mileage are separate opt-ins. Bluetooth telemetry requires compatible BLE hardware and vehicle support.

Weather and safe use

Radar depends on network access and provider coverage; check the displayed observation time. It is informational and may be delayed. Use interactive features as a passenger or while safely parked.
