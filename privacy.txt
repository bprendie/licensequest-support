Decker’s LicenseQuest Privacy Policy
Effective November 14, 2026

Decker’s LicenseQuest (displayed as LicenseQuest in some versions) is provided by Robert Prendergast. Contact: bprendie@icloud.com.

Your collection
LicenseQuest records the state, province or other jurisdiction you choose, not a vehicle's registration number, photograph, owner or identity. Collections, trip records, scores, roadside finds and preferences are stored on your device. The app has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking or developer-operated data server. iOS device backups may include local app data according to your settings. The Android app disables cloud app backup; device-to-device transfer behavior can depend on the operating system. Uninstalling it can remove your collection.

Optional location
With permission, location suggests a starting city and can estimate mileage when you enable GPS distance. Coarse home and trip anchors are saved for regional scoring. The app does not save a route trail. Background location is used only when you explicitly enable background mileage for a trip. You can stop a trip or revoke location permission in system Settings. On iOS, city lookup uses Apple’s geocoding service, which can receive the coordinates or search text needed to answer the request. The current Android release uses a city database bundled with the app: city searches and nearby-city suggestions are processed on your device without sending coordinates or search text to a geocoding service. Nearby-city suggestions are estimates, not proof that you are inside a city boundary. Exact locations are not sent to the developer. Mileage calculations stay on the device. Android background mileage uses an ongoing notification with a Pause mileage action.

Optional speech
On iOS, tapping Voice/Talk requests microphone and speech permission; on-device recognition is requested where supported, otherwise Apple’s speech service may process audio. On Android, Voice opens the device’s speech-recognition provider. Offline recognition is requested, but the provider may process audio online according to its settings and availability. LicenseQuest does not save audio recordings or send them to the developer. Confirmed region selections become local collection entries. Touch controls work without speech permission.

Earlier vehicle records
Version 1.1 on iOS and Android uses GPS mileage and no longer requests Bluetooth access. Historical trip distance records remain on your device; the update does not convert old distances into new mileage points.

Collectible artwork
Crew artwork and plate unlocks are stored locally. Sharing an unlocked image uses the system share sheet and sends only the content you choose to your selected destination.

Optional weather
When radar is enabled, the app requests weather images from Environment and Climate Change Canada's GeoMet service. The service receives your IP address and the requested map bounds and times. Bounds can reflect your selected home, trip start or explored area. The app caches weather data on your device and does not attach an account or advertising identifier. Android weather requests use HTTPS. Weather, iOS geocoding and speech providers operate under their own privacy policies. These optional requests are not tied to a LicenseQuest account because the app has no accounts.

Offline city-data credit
The Android city database is adapted from GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). We adapted the cities500, admin1CodesASCII and admin2Codes extracts to include the United States, Canada, Mexico and Puerto Rico, filter place names and aliases, add app jurisdiction identifiers and county/district labels, and store the result in a compact local format. City points are not municipal boundaries. GeoNames data may be incomplete or outdated and GeoNames does not endorse this app.

Support
If you email support, we receive the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send and use them to answer your request. Avoid sending private location or vehicle details unless needed. Request removal of support correspondence by emailing the same address, subject to necessary record retention.

Control and retention
App records remain local until you remove them or remove the app's data. Undo controls remove individual eligible sightings or finds from active progress; history can retain reversal records. Removing the app can remove its local records, and device backups may retain earlier copies. No developer account is created and there is no developer-side collection database to delete.

Changes
Updates to this policy will appear here with a new effective date. Questions: bprendie@icloud.com.
